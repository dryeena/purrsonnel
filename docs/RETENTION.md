# Initial privacy and retention policy draft

This is a proposal for organizational review, not an assertion of statutory compliance. All seeded names, IDs, answers, notes, and policies are fictional.

Requests retain message text and recipient snapshots for authorized participants. Push endpoints and device encryption keys are private server-side operational records. Lock-screen payloads contain only a generic alert and a request link. Opting out disables the endpoint and pending jobs; 404/410 responses deactivate stale endpoints. Delivery jobs expire after one hour, but physical deletion and an approved retention period for request/device history still need a retention policy and cleanup job. Public offline assets contain no personnel data; application/API responses are never cached by the service worker.

The current implementation avoids collecting signature IP addresses, user agents, camera images, local passwords, and external identity tokens. Camera frames remain on-device and are discarded when the scanner closes. QR URLs contain only a random opaque reference; the database stores hashes. QR references expire after ten minutes. Sessions expire after twelve hours; expired sessions are cleaned during new session establishment. Expired QR records currently remain in the database until a retention job is implemented.

Proposed policy for review: minimize application data, retain declined applications for a defined recruiting window approved by FWA, retain volunteer time and terms evidence for an approved operational/legal period, restrict interview notes to the assigned audience, and retain audit records for an approved accountability period. Do not choose retention durations without the responsible organization’s approval.

Administrators should handle correction, CSV exports, and deactivation requests through audited workflows. Directory exports exclude legal name/email by default and neutralize spreadsheet formulas. The sensitive-permission-gated person-record export includes identity, directory, explicit service years, custom fields, terms evidence, volunteer entries, and applications; it is audited. It does not yet reconcile every historical relationship, interview record, and retained event into a comprehensive subject-access response. Deactivation changes account status; it does not erase historical decisions or signed documents. A retention/purge job has not yet been implemented. Before real personal data is loaded, approve retention durations, implement scheduled cleanup and a comprehensive subject export, validate access roles, and document restoration/deletion behavior for backups.
